# Company Scenario: Müller Maschinenbau GmbH

## 1. Company Profile
- **Name:** Müller Maschinenbau GmbH *(Fictional)*
- **Industry:** Industrial Machinery & Precision Manufacturing (German *Mittelstand*)
- **Location:** Germany
- **Headcount:** ~150 employees
- **IT & OT Landscape:**
  - **On-Premise:** Active Directory, local file servers, ERP system.
  - **Cloud / SaaS:** Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online, SharePoint, Teams), external backup provider.
  - **Production / OT:** CNC machinery, shop-floor terminals, and industrial control networks partially connected to corporate IT.

---

## 2. Role & Governance
In this simulation, I act as the **IT-Sicherheitsbeauftragter (ISB)** responsible for driving the NIS2 readiness assessment and building the ISMS structure based on BSI IT-Grundschutz.

Rather than working in isolation, the project simulates realistic interactions and operational friction with four key internal and external stakeholders:

| Stakeholder | Role in Simulation | Typical Operational Friction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Geschäftsführung** | Executive Management | Approves budget and accepts residual risks; requires concise, business-oriented reporting without technical jargon. |
| **IT-Leiter** | Head of IT Operations | Manages servers and M365; time-constrained, often assumes controls are "already in place" without formal evidence. |
| **Produktionsleiter** | Head of Production / OT | Prioritizes manufacturing uptime; skeptical of security measures that could disrupt shop-floor availability. |
| **External IT / Cloud Vendor** | Third-Party Service Provider | Slow to provide security attestations; occasionally relies on legacy practices (e.g., shared administrative accounts). |
